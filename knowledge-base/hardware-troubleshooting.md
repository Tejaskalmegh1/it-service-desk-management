# Knowledge Base: Basic Hardware & Peripheral Troubleshooting

**Article ID:** KB-HW-001  
**Category:** Hardware  
**Audience:** L1 support practice

## Scenario: Keyboard, mouse, or USB device is not detected

### Initial questions

- Which device and port are affected?
- Is the device wired or wireless?
- Does it work on another approved computer?
- Did the issue start after a drop, spill, update, or physical change?

### Safe first-line checks

1. Check for visible damage, loose connections, or depleted batteries.
2. Reconnect the device using an approved port.
3. For wireless devices, check power and pairing status.
4. Test another known-good port or approved peripheral, if available.
5. On Windows, inspect Device Manager for a relevant warning.
6. Restart only after saving work and confirming it is appropriate.
7. Record the device, port tested, observed status, and outcome.

### Safety

- Disconnect equipment if there is smoke, burning smell, unusual heat, liquid damage, or exposed wiring.
- Do not open power supplies, monitors, or other components that require qualified repair.
- Do not install unapproved drivers.

### Escalate when

- Physical damage or electrical risk is present.
- Multiple ports or devices fail.
- Hardware replacement or administrator-level driver work is required.
